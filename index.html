HyperX Player - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2024-08-XX

1. Overview

HyperX Player is a simple media player application. It plays videos and music from:

  • Your device storage (local files)
  • Online streams and IPTV playlists that you enter manually

We do not provide, host, or sell any media content. All content is loaded either from your device or from URLs that you choose.

2. Data We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or upload any personal data to our own servers.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords
  • Location data
  • Analytics identifiers or advertising IDs

The app only accesses:

  • Media files on your device, to list and play them
  • Network URLs that you enter, to stream media from those links

3. Permissions

The app uses the following Android permissions only for core playback features:

  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android):
    Used to scan and show your local videos and music. Files are processed only on your device.
  • INTERNET:
    Used to play online streams from URLs you enter.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK:
    Used to keep music playback running in the background with notification controls.

We do not use these permissions to collect or track personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

HyperX Player uses standard Android libraries (ExoPlayer / Media3, Glide, Material Components) only for:

  • Media playback
  • Image loading
  • UI components

We do not use any third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics) or third-party advertising SDKs in this app.

5. IPTV and Online Streams

You are responsible for the URLs and IPTV playlists you add to the app.

  • HyperX Player does not provide or pre-load any copyrighted channels, movies, or playlists.
  • We are not responsible for the availability, legality, or content of any third-party streams.
  • Please make sure you have the right to view the content you stream.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with any third parties. We do not upload your media library to any server.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general use and does not specifically target children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated on this page and, if applicable, inside the app and on the Google Play Store listing.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via the email address provided on our Google Play Store listing.
